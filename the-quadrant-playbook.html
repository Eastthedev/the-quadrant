What is
The Quadrant

The Quadrant is a copytrade amplification system. It takes a signal from a trusted trader — their entry, stop loss, and take profit — and rather than copying it as-is, it restructures the trade into four independent entries spread across four separate accounts.

The core idea is this: the SL region of any trade is not a wall, it's a zone. That zone has depth. Price doesn't always hit SL directly — it often probes, dips, and reverses. The Quadrant exploits that depth by placing staggered limit entries at each quarter of the SL zone, turning one signal into up to four positions with dramatically improved risk-to-reward ratios.

Every account targets the same take profit. Only the entries and stop losses differ. The deeper price dips before reversing, the fewer accounts get filled — but the ones that do carry outsized R:R. Missed entries are not losses. They are simply unfilled orders.

// Core insight

A trader with a 1:2 setup and a 60% win rate is profitable. The same signal, restructured through The Quadrant, gives you 1:8, 1:9, 1:10, and 1:11 across four accounts. You can be profitable at a win rate a fraction of theirs.

// 02

The Logic

Given any signal, three values are extracted: Entry (E), Stop Loss (SL), and Take Profit (TP). The distance from entry to stop loss is the SL range. This range is divided into four equal zones.

zone_size = (Entry − SL) ÷ 4

Q1_entry = Entry            // original signal entry
Q1_sl    = Entry − zone_size

Q2_entry = Q1_sl            // Q2 enters where Q1's SL is
Q2_sl    = Q2_entry − zone_size

Q3_entry = Q2_sl
Q3_sl    = Q3_entry − zone_size

Q4_entry = Q3_sl
Q4_sl    = Original SL     // Q4 holds the full stop

// All quadrants share the same TP
Q1_tp = Q2_tp = Q3_tp = Q4_tp = TP

Because every quadrant has the same zone size as its risk, but each successive quadrant is closer to the TP, the reward distance grows while risk stays constant. This is why R:R improves with each deeper quadrant.

What happens
on each trade

Price moves one of five ways after a signal is issued. Here is how The Quadrant responds to each outcome:

Price behaviour Q1 Q2 Q3 Q4
Goes straight to TP from entry +41R no fill no fill no fill
Dips to Q2 zone, then to TP −1R +42R no fill no fill
Dips to Q3 zone, then to TP −1R −1R +43R no fill
Dips to Q4 zone, then to TP −1R −1R −1R +44R
Blows through full SL −1R −1R −1R −1R

In the worst case — full stop out on all four accounts — you lose 4R total. In any winning scenario, a single filled account more than compensates. One Q1 win returns 41R, covering 41 full losses. The mathematics of this system are structurally biased in your favour as long as your signal source maintains any meaningful edge.

// Break-even win rate on Q1

At 1:41 R:R, Q1 breaks even at a win rate of just 2.4%. Your friend's signals almost certainly win more than 2.4% of the time. Every percentage above that is pure edge compounding across four accounts.

Risk
Management

The Quadrant is designed primarily for prop firm accounts with a 10% maximum drawdown limit. The risk sizing layer ensures you never approach that ceiling while staying active and compounding when healthy.

// Green mode
At or above breakeven
Risk 1% of account per trade on each account. Full exposure. You are operating from a position of strength — let the system work at full capacity.
// Recovery mode
In drawdown
Drop to 0.5% per trade per account. Conserve capital. Smaller size means the same R:R ratios still recover the account — just more slowly and safely.
// Hard floor
10% drawdown limit
This is the prop firm's hard limit. The risk sizing tiers above are designed so this ceiling is never approached under normal trading conditions.
// Account structure
4 independent accounts
Each quadrant lives on a separate account. A loss on Q1 does not affect Q2's drawdown. Accounts are isolated — a bad run on one does not contaminate the others.

The isolation of accounts is not just structural convenience — it is a risk management feature. If Q1 hits a rough streak and enters recovery mode at 0.5%, Q2, Q3, and Q4 may still be in green mode at 1%. The system self-balances.

With four accounts each risking 1% on a signal, the maximum combined exposure per signal is 4% across all accounts. In the best-case scenario where all four fill and TP is hit, the return is enormous. In the worst case, it is a controlled 4R loss — manageable, recoverable, and absorbed by the R:R ratios over any reasonable sample of trades.

How to
Build It

The Quadrant is built in three phases. Each phase is independently functional — you can start with Phase 1 and generate real value immediately while building toward the full system.

01
The Calculator
A web app where you input Entry, SL, and TP manually. It instantly computes all four quadrant levels — entry prices, stop losses, and R:R for each account. This is the core engine everything else is built on.
  • Input: Entry, SL, TP, account balance, risk %
  • Output: Q1–Q4 entry prices and SL levels
  • Output: R:R ratio per quadrant
  • Output: Position size in lots/units per account
  • Output: Dollar value at risk per account
  • Risk mode toggle: green (1%) vs recovery (0.5%)
02
The Image Extractor
When your signal source shares a chart screenshot, you upload it directly to The Quadrant app. AI vision reads the price levels from the chart — entry line, SL zone, TP zone — and feeds them automatically into the calculator. No manual input.
  • Upload chart screenshot from Telegram, WhatsApp, or anywhere
  • Claude vision API identifies entry, SL, and TP price levels
  • Handles annotated charts, shaded zones, and horizontal lines
  • Confidence score shown — flag ambiguous images for manual review
  • Works best when price axis is visible on right side of chart
  • Auto-detects pair name and timeframe where readable
03
The Order Manager
The full system. Connects to your broker accounts via API. When a signal is processed, it places all four limit orders automatically across all four accounts — correct size, correct entry, correct SL and TP per quadrant. Manages open trades and tracks performance.
  • Broker API integration (MetaTrader 5, cTrader, or broker REST API)
  • One-click or fully automated order placement across 4 accounts
  • Real-time position tracking per account
  • Auto risk mode detection — switches to recovery at drawdown threshold
  • Trade journal with per-quadrant performance breakdown
  • TradingView deep links for chart verification before execution

Rules of
The Quadrant

These are the operating principles. They are not suggestions — they are what makes the system work over time.

I
Never move a SL
Each quadrant's SL is mathematically precise. Moving it invalidates the R:R calculation and breaks the logic of the system. Let the trade work or let it stop out.
II
Never skip a quadrant
The system works because all four positions are placed. Selectively skipping quadrants based on feeling removes the statistical edge. Place all four, every time.
III
Missed entries are not losses
If price goes straight to TP without filling Q2, Q3, Q4 — that is a good outcome. Do not chase unfilled orders. A missed entry costs nothing.
IV
Respect the risk tiers
1% in green mode, 0.5% in recovery. Never increase size chasing losses. The R:R ratios do the heavy lifting — your job is to stay in the game long enough for them to compound.
V
Only copy traders with a verified edge
The Quadrant amplifies an existing edge — it does not create one from nothing. The signal source must have a documented win rate over a meaningful sample size. Vet your sources before running the system on their signals.
