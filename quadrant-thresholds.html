The core break-even formula

For any trading system to be profitable, expected value must be positive. The formula is simple: wins must outweigh losses over time. The Quadrant doesn't change this fundamental law — it amplifies it. Here is the base formula applied to Q1.

// Break-even win rate formula
BE_winrate = 1 ÷ (1 + R:R)

// Example: signal provider gives 1:2 R:R
Q1 zone = SL ÷ 4                // risk is now 25% of original
Q1 reward = TP − Q1_entry     // same TP, closer entry = more reward
Q1 R:R at original 1:2 = 1:8  // 4× amplification on R:R

BE_winrate on Q1 = 1 ÷ (1 + 8) = 11.1%

// If provider wins more than 11.1% of trades → Q1 is profitable
// A provider with 1:2 and 34% win rate gives you massive margin above 11.1%

This is the structural power of The Quadrant. A signal provider only needs to be barely profitable themselves — and the system transforms their marginal edge into a substantial one on your end. Their break-even at 1:2 is 33.3%. Yours on Q1 is 11.1%.

// the amplification rule

Whatever break-even win rate the signal provider needs, The Quadrant cuts your required win rate to roughly one quarter of theirs — because your R:R is approximately 4× theirs on Q1. Their 33% becomes your 11%. Their 20% becomes your 5%.

// 05

The win rate that kills the system

For each R:R level a provider offers, here is the exact win rate at which The Quadrant becomes unprofitable on Q1. This is your disqualification line for any signal source.

// Unprofitable threshold per provider R:R

Provider 1:1 R:R   → Q1 is 1:4   → unprofitable below 20% win rate
Provider 1:2 R:R   → Q1 is 1:8   → unprofitable below 11.1% win rate
Provider 1:3 R:R   → Q1 is 1:12 → unprofitable below 7.7% win rate
Provider 1:5 R:R   → Q1 is 1:20 → unprofitable below 4.8% win rate
Provider 1:10 R:R  → Q1 is 1:41 → unprofitable below 2.4% win rate

// The rule: as R:R goes up, the kill threshold drops fast
// A 1:10 provider who wins 3% of trades still runs a profitable Q1
// A 1:1 provider needs 21% to avoid losses — and most can't sustain that

The practical implication: prioritise signal providers with high R:R over high win rate. A provider with 1:5 R:R and a 20% win rate is far more valuable to The Quadrant than a provider with 1:1.5 R:R and 55% win rate. The former gives you 1:20 on Q1 and a 4.8% break-even. The latter gives you 1:6 and a 14.3% break-even.

Disqualifying: any provider with negative EV over 50+ trades. If a provider's own results are net negative over a meaningful sample, The Quadrant cannot save it. Verify provider track records before running the system on their signals.

The Quadrant turns a signal provider's 1:3 R:R into your 1:12 — meaning a signal source only needs to be right one in thirteen times for you to break even. With any decent provider, you are structurally guaranteed to be profitable over time.
